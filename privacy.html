Privacy policy for Jantar Mantar Rush

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Jantar Mantar Rush ("the game") is published by NRM Studio and developed by Naman, Delhi, India ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account. We do not run our own servers for the game. We only see anonymous, combined usage statistics from Google Analytics for Firebase (see below), which do not tell us who you are.

Information stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, coins, unlocked characters and settings are saved only on your phone. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads. Ads may be personalised. Google's use of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, for example how many people play each day, which levels they reach, and whether the game runs well. It collects app usage events (such as level started or finished), a random app instance ID, your device's advertising ID, device model, Android version, approximate location (country/city from IP address) and diagnostic information. This data is processed by Google and used only to improve the game. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy for details.

Permissions

Internet and network state: to load ads and send analytics. Vibration: for in-game feedback. You can turn vibration off in the game's settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.
